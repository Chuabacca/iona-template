One-paragraph summary of the sermon's argument: God sovereignly works all
things together for the good of His called people, guaranteed by the unbroken
chain from foreknowledge to glorification.

## Exegesis

- `[ROM.8.28]` "Work together" (sunergeo) — God cooperating all circumstances toward one end; the verse claims divine agency in all things, not goodness of all things › [¶3 "means to work together"]
- `[ROM.8.29-30]` Five aorists form an unbroken sequence from foreknowledge to glorification; the certainty of the promise in v28 rests on this chain › [¶4 "an unbreakable chain of salvation"]

## Application

- `[ROM.8.28]` Trust under suffering does not require seeing the reasons; the promise is God's working, not our understanding of it › [¶5 "you do not have to know the reasons"]

## History

- Verse 28 described as widely loved and widely misquoted — reception history worth tracing › [¶1 "most loved and most misquoted"]

## Questions

- `[ROM.8.28]` Does "them that love God" restrict the promise's scope, and how does the speaker relate it to "the called"? › [¶2 "to them who are the called"]
