This morning we come to Romans chapter 8, and I want you to look with me at verse 28, one of the most loved and most misquoted promises in all of Scripture.

And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to his purpose. The apostle says we know — not we hope, not we wish, not we feel. We know.

The key word here, sunergeo, means to work together, to cooperate toward a single end. Paul is not saying every thing is good; he is saying God is at work in all things, weaving them together for the good of His people.

Then verses 29 and 30: for whom he did foreknow, he also did predestinate to be conformed to the image of his Son. Foreknown, predestined, called, justified, glorified — an unbreakable chain of salvation, with no link missing and no one lost between the links.

So what does this mean for you? When suffering comes, you do not have to know the reasons to trust the Redeemer. The promise is not that you will see the good, but that God is working it.
