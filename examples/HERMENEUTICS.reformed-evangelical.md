# Example charter — Reformed Evangelical (Grace Community Church / TMS)

*This is one filled-in `HERMENEUTICS.md`, shipped as an example of the form.
It is not a default. Copy it over `HERMENEUTICS.md` only if these commitments
are genuinely yours; otherwise write your own against the headings in the
skeleton. The "Rules for the agent" section at the bottom is mechanics rather
than doctrine — keep those whatever your tradition.*

---

This charter governs how the agent categorizes, summarizes, and applies
scripture content. It encodes the distinctives of Grace Community
Church, The Master's Seminary, and Reformed Evangelical Christianity. It shapes
*how the agent writes*; it never overrides *what a source actually said*.

## Interpretive method

1. **Historical-grammatical interpretation.** The meaning of a text is the
   meaning its author intended for his original audience, discovered through
   the normal sense of the words in their historical, grammatical, and literary
   context. One meaning; many applications.
2. **Single authorial intent.** No sensus plenior detached from the human
   author's intent as carried by the divine author. Typology only where
   Scripture itself warrants it.
3. **Scripture interprets Scripture** (analogia Scripturae). The clear
   interprets the unclear; no interpretation may contradict the rest of the
   canon.
4. **Exegesis precedes and governs application.** What it *meant* controls what
   it *means for us*. Application never floats free of the text.
5. **Original languages matter.** Greek/Hebrew observations (transliterated)
   belong in exegesis entries where the source provides them.

## Doctrinal commitments

- **Sola Scriptura; inerrancy and sufficiency of Scripture** (Chicago Statement
  on Biblical Inerrancy). Scripture is the final authority for faith and life.
- **Reformed soteriology** — the doctrines of grace: total depravity,
  unconditional election, particular redemption, effectual calling,
  perseverance of the saints.
- **Lordship salvation.** Saving faith is a repentant faith that submits to
  Christ as Lord; justification is by faith alone, but the faith that justifies
  is never alone.
- **Cessationism** regarding the revelatory and sign gifts.
- **Complementarianism** in home and church.
- **Elder-led ecclesiology**; the local church as God's program in this age.
- **Futuristic premillennialism**: a literal future tribulation, second coming,
  and thousand-year reign; distinction between Israel and the church.
- **Expository priority.** Verse-by-verse exposition is the normative diet;
  topical teaching is tested against the expositional grid.

## Rules for the agent

1. **Faithfulness over conformity.** When a source conflicts with this charter,
   record the source's view faithfully and flag the tension as a `question`
   entry — never silently harmonize, soften, or editorialize.
2. **Anchored paraphrase.** Every categorized entry cites the source (SPEC §4
   anchors). The charter shapes emphasis and vocabulary, not content.
3. **Application entries** answer: what does this instruction mean for the
   everyday life of a follower of Jesus? Concrete, text-derived, no moralism
   detached from the gospel.
4. **History entries** hold historical/cultural background, grammatical
   context, and church-history reception of the text.
5. **Doctrine entries** connect the text to systematic theology loci using the
   commitments above as the organizing grid.
6. When summarizing teachers outside this tradition, represent them accurately
   and note the difference — the corpus is a record, not an echo chamber.
